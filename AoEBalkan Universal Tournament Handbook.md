## 1. Introduction

### About AoEBalkan

AoEBalkan is a community of Age of Empires 2: Definitive Edition players from the Balkan region (ex-Yugoslav countries) and friends. Our Discord server has ~700 members with 50–100 casually active players and a core group of ~30 people who have been playing together for over 5 years. We run our own tournaments, play lobby games nightly, and have even organized IRL LAN gatherings.

### Purpose of This Handbook

This handbook exists to:

- **Prevent organizational mistakes** that have happened in past events
- **Provide a universal foundation** that any community member can use when organizing a tournament
- **Ensure fair, balanced, and enjoyable competition** for all skill levels
- **Save time** — organizers don't need to write rules from scratch

This document is inspired by the [Official AoE2 Tournament Handbook](https://www.ageofempires.com/news/tournament_handbook_ageiide/) and adapted for our community's needs.

### How to Use This Handbook

When organizing a tournament, start your announcement with:

> *"This tournament follows the AoEBalkan Universal Tournament Handbook with the following changes/additions: [list any specific modifications]"*

## 2. Organizer Checklist

Before announcing a tournament, verify you have addressed:

### Pre-Announcement (at least 1-2 weeks before signups)

- [ ] **Format defined**: 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 4v4? Single elimination, double elimination, round robin, Swiss?
- [ ] **Series lengths defined**: BO1, BO3, BO5, BO7 for each stage
- [ ] **Map pool finalized**: All maps tested and working
- [ ] **Map draft preset created**: Using [aoe2cm.net](https://aoe2cm.net)
- [ ] **Civ draft preset created**: With appropriate bans/snipes
- [ ] **Timeline established**: Registration period, group stage dates, playoff dates, finals date
- [ ] **Prize pool (if any)**: Amount, distribution percentages, and payment method announced upfront
- [ ] **Admin team**: At least 1-2 people available to help during tournament

### Before Tournament Start

- [ ] **Seeding completed**: Using consistent, transparent methodology
- [ ] **Brackets/groups published**: On Challonge, start.gg, or Toornament
- [ ] **Map pack published**: In AoE2 mod center (if using custom maps)
- [ ] **Communication channels ready**: Discord channels for scheduling, results, etc.
- [ ] **Rules document finalized**: Any tournament-specific modifications documented

### Custom Map Requirements

**Custom maps MUST be tested before inclusion.** At minimum:

- [ ] Play at least 2-3 test games on each custom map
- [ ] Verify all starting resources spawn correctly
- [ ] Verify no resources are trapped or inaccessible
- [ ] Verify map balance (both spawns should be roughly equivalent)
- [ ] Document any special mechanics or settings required
- [ ] If a map has issues, either fix it or remove it from the pool

> ⚠️ **Never include untested custom maps in a tournament.** This has caused problems in the past.

## 3. Registration & Seeding

### Registration Requirements

- Players must register with their **primary account** (highest rated, most active, most known)
- Players should have at least **20 ranked 1v1 games** played to help with accurate seeding
- Smurf accounts will not be accepted
- Players should not change their in-game name during the tournament

### Seeding Methods

Choose one method and apply it consistently:

#### For 1v1 Tournaments

**Recommended**: Sum of (current 1v1 Elo) + (peak 1v1 Elo) from [aoe2insights.com](https://aoe2insights.com)

- Account should have a reasonable number of games for accurate rating



#### For Team Game Tournaments

**Recommended**: Sum of (current 1v1 Elo + peak 1v1 Elo + current TG Elo + peak TG Elo) per player.

- Use the 4 highest-rated players for team seeding (even if roster is larger)
- Manual adjustments allowed if Elo clearly doesn't reflect skill

### Seeding Transparency

- Publish seeding methodology before tournament starts
- Allow players to dispute obvious errors before groups are drawn
- If a lower-seeded player defeats a higher-seeded player, they inherit that seed for subsequent rounds

### Established Players Without Recent Ranked Activity

For players with established names in AoEBalkan who have played in several previous tournaments or have been active in the community for a long time, organizers may use alternative criteria beyond just Elo. This is especially relevant for:

- Players who don't play ranked games regularly
- TG-only players participating in team game tournaments
- Players whose current Elo doesn't reflect their actual skill level

In such cases, admins may use community knowledge, past tournament performance, or peer assessment to adjust seeding.

## 4. Scheduling & Punctuality

### Communication

- Tournament communication is expected in **Balkan languages** (Bosnian, Serbian, Croatian, Montenegrin)
- If someone doesn't speak these languages, use English or another common language
- Players must respond to scheduling messages within **24 hours**
- All match times and drafts must be posted in the designated Discord channel

### Tournament Player Voice Channels

- Players registered for AoEBalkan tournaments will be assigned the **Tournament Player** role
- This role grants access to special voice channels designated for tournament matches
- **Players must be present in these voice channels when playing their tournament matches**
- This allows admins to quickly join and communicate if issues arise
- Faster issue resolution and clearer communication benefits everyone

### Discord Roles for Tournament Organization

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Event Organiser** | Members actively involved in organizing tournaments |
| **Cavalier** | Server moderators — can assist with tournament issues |
| **Paladin** | Server admins — highest role below server founder |

If you need help during a tournament, contact anyone with these roles.

### Scheduling Matches

- Players should contact opponents as early as possible
- Provide multiple available time slots, not just one
- Players are encouraged to schedule matches with at least **24 hours notice**
- Use [Crab Fit](https://crab.fit/) to find common availability
- Use [Discord Timestamps](https://r.3v.fi/discord-timestamps/) for timezone-proof times
- **After scheduling, notify an admin** to create a Seshbot event for the match time — this is mainly for matches that will be casted, but it's good to add events for any match so others know when to watch

### Punctuality

- Players must be ready to start at the scheduled time
- **For every 10 minutes late without notice**, the late player loses 1 game of the set
- **20+ minutes late without notice**: Opponent wins the set, unless they agree to reschedule
- If both players are late, work it out between yourselves or contact an admin

### Sets Must Be Played Completely

Once a set begins, **all games must be played in one session** (same day, consecutively). Don't start a BO5 if you don't have time to finish it.

### Deadlines

- Teams/players that fall **2+ weeks behind schedule** will be disqualified
- If a player cannot play by the deadline, contact admins immediately for potential extensions

## 5. Map Pool Requirements

### General Requirements

- Map pools should contain **7-11 maps** for typical tournaments
- Maps should offer **variety** in playstyle (open, closed, hybrid, water)
- All maps must be tested and verified working on current patch

### Standard (Official) Maps

Prefer official maps or well-known community maps (e.g., KotD Arabia versions). These are already balanced and tested.

### Custom Maps

If using custom maps:

1. **Testing is mandatory** — minimum 2-3 games per map before tournament
2. **Document any special settings** (e.g., "play on Explored", "Regicide mode")
3. **Publish the map pack** in the AoE2 mod center at least **1 week before** tournament start
4. **Provide screenshots** or descriptions of intended map generation
5. **Have a backup plan** — if a custom map has issues mid-tournament, what happens?

### What to Check When Testing Maps

- Starting resources spawn correctly for both players
- No resources are trapped or inaccessible
- Relics spawn in the correct quantity
- No villagers or units spawn trapped
- Woodlines, gold, stone, berries are gatherable
- Map generation is roughly balanced between spawns

## 6. Map Draft Rules

### Core Requirement

> **Every map draft MUST include at least one form of opponent denial (ban).**

This ensures players cannot be forced onto maps that hard-counter their playstyle.

### Draft Tool

Use [aoe2cm.net](https://aoe2cm.net) for all drafts. Create a preset and share the link in your tournament rules.

### Who Hosts the Draft

- **Higher seed hosts both drafts** (map draft first, then civ draft)
- In playoffs: the player/team with better tournament placement hosts
- If equal, original seed determines host

### Recommended Draft Formats

#### BO3 Map Draft (9 map pool)
```
Host bans 2 → Guest bans 2 → Host picks 1 → Guest picks 1 → Random from remaining = neutral map
```

#### BO5 Map Draft (9 map pool)
```
Host bans 1 → Guest bans 1 → Host picks 2 → Guest picks 2 → Random from remaining = neutral map
```

#### BO7 Map Draft (11 map pool)
```
Host bans 1 → Guest bans 1 → Host picks 3 → Guest picks 3 → Random from remaining = neutral map
```

### Map Order During the Set

**Option A (Recommended for BO3 groups)**: Play all maps in order (neutral → lower seed pick → higher seed pick)

**Option B (Recommended for playoffs)**: Neutral map first, then **loser of previous game picks next map** from their drafted maps.

### Home Maps vs. Neutral Maps

- **Home map**: A map the player picked for themselves
- **Neutral map**: Random or blind-picked, neither player chose it
- Consider giving slight server preference to home map player if pings are unequal

## 7. Civilization Draft Rules

### Core Requirement

> **Every civ draft MUST include opponent denial — at minimum 2 global bans per player.**

Given AoE2's current balance state, civ bans are essential to fair competition.

### Why Bans Are Mandatory

- Some civilizations are significantly overtuned on certain maps
- Without bans, drafts become "pick the OP civ" exercises
- Bans add strategic depth and reward preparation

### Draft Tool

Use [aoe2cm.net](https://aoe2cm.net). Create presets that match your series length.

### Draft Actions Explained

| Action | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Ban** | Neither player can pick this civ for any game |
| **Pick** | This civ goes into your pool; you can choose to play it in one game |
| **Snipe** | You remove a civ your opponent already picked (they lose it from their pool) |
| **Steal** | You take a civ your opponent picked and add it to your own pool |

### Recommended Draft Formats

#### BO3 Civ Draft
```
Global bans: Host bans 2 → Guest bans 2
Picks: Alternating picks until each player has 3 civs
```

#### BO5 Civ Draft  
```
Global bans: Host bans 3 → Guest bans 3
Picks: Alternating picks until each player has 5 civs
Optional: Include 1 snipe per player
```

#### BO7 Civ Draft
```
Global bans: Host bans 3 → Guest bans 3
Picks: Alternating picks until each player has 7 civs
Optional: Include 1-2 snipes per player
```

### Civ Draft Rules

- **No civ repeats**: A player cannot play the same civilization twice in a set
- Players may only pick civs they own (have DLC for)
- If a player picks a civ they cannot play (banned, already used, or don't own), the opponent chooses which available civ they must play instead

### DLC Civilizations

- All currently released DLC civs are permitted unless specified otherwise
- If a new DLC releases during a tournament, admins will announce whether it's allowed
- Players should only draft civs they personally own

## 9. In-Game Rules

### Legal Actions

Players may do anything the game allows, including:

- Walling in resources, relics, or neutral objects
- Killing enemy villagers, herdables, or wild animals
- Tower rushing, castle dropping, or any aggressive strategy
- Any creative or unconventional strategy

### Illegal Actions

- **Cheats or hacks** of any kind — instant disqualification
- **Macros** (external software that emulates multiple keypresses) — instant disqualification
- **Stream sniping** — watching opponent's stream during the game
- **Outside assistance** — receiving help from spectators, stream chat, or voice chat with non-teammates
- **Intentional bugs/exploits** — using known game-breaking exploits

### Graphical Mods

- Graphical mods from the official mod center are allowed
- Small trees, grid terrain, and similar visual aids are permitted
- No mods that provide gameplay advantage (e.g., revealing hidden information)

## 10. Laming Rules

### Definition

"Laming" refers to early-game harassment tactics that disrupt the opponent's economy without direct military engagement, such as:

- Stealing or killing enemy sheep/boar
- Walling in enemy resources
- Killing enemy deer or other hunt
- Scout harassment

### Default Rule: Laming Allowed

**By default, laming is permitted** on standard start maps. This includes:

- Stealing enemy herdables (sheep, boar, deer)
- Killing enemy herdables
- Walling in enemy resources
- Any early aggression

### Optional: No-Laming Rule

Organizers may choose to implement a **no-laming rule** for specific tournaments. If so, clearly define:

1. What actions are prohibited
2. The time window (e.g., "first 5 minutes" or "until Feudal Age")
3. The penalty for violation (usually game loss)

Example no-laming rule:
> *"Laming is prohibited in Dark Age. Players may not steal, kill, or wall in opponent's starting resources (sheep, boar, berries, deer) until at least one player reaches Feudal Age. Violation results in game loss."*

### Tournament-Specific Laming Rules Must Be Clear

If your tournament modifies laming rules, state it explicitly. Don't leave it ambiguous.

## 11. Nomad-Specific Rules

> **Note**: These rules only apply to **custom nomad maps that disable the built-in Nomad Treaty**. Default nomad maps in the game (like Nomad, Land Nomad, etc.) have a built-in treaty that handles these restrictions automatically — these manual rules are not needed for those maps.

On custom nomad maps without built-in treaty, the following rules apply during the **first 3 minutes** of in-game time:

### Prohibited Actions (First 3 Minutes)

- ❌ Attacking enemy units, buildings, or herdables
- ❌ Walling in enemy units, buildings, or resources
- ❌ Walling in relics
- ❌ Building your first TC within firing range of enemy TC
- ❌ Killing wild animals without gathering at least 35 food from the carcass

### Permitted Actions (First 3 Minutes)

- ✅ Stealing enemy herdables (taking control without killing)
- ✅ Scouting enemy positions
- ✅ Normal economic activities

### TC Placement

If two players accidentally build their TCs in range of each other, the game must be **immediately restarted**.

### After 3 Minutes

After 3 minutes, all normal rules apply. Laming, attacking, and aggressive play are fully permitted.

## 12. Restarts

### Player Restarts

- Each player receives **1 free restart** per set (BO3, BO5, etc.)
- Free restarts may only be called in the **first 5 minutes** of in-game time
- No reason needs to be given for the free restart
- After using the free restart, additional restarts require admin approval

### Admin Restarts (Bugged Maps)

Admin restarts do not count against player's free restart. They are granted when:

- More than 25% of a resource (gold, stone, berries) is inaccessible
- Starting resources fail to generate correctly
- Villagers or units spawn trapped
- Relics fail to generate in correct quantity
- Herdables or wild animals are trapped or inaccessible
- Any major map generation failure affecting fairness

### How to Request a Restart

1. **Pause the game immediately**
2. **Contact an admin** if available; otherwise, discuss with opponent
3. **Document the issue** (screenshot if possible)
4. If no admin is available and players disagree, play continues and admin reviews the replay

### After a Restart

- **Same map** must be played
- **Same civilizations** must be used
- Players may not reveal the map or watch the replay before rehosting

## 13. Pausing & Disconnections

### Pausing Rules

- Pause only for **technical issues** (lag, connection problems, hardware issues)
- Do not pause for strategic reasons (checking tech tree, thinking about decisions)
- Notify opponent immediately when pausing
- The player who paused must unpause
- Confirm opponent is ready before unpausing

### Excessive Pausing

Repeated pausing without valid reason will result in warnings and potential penalties.

### Disconnections & Crashes

1. If the game crashes or desyncs, **exit without revealing the map**
2. Attempt to **restore the game** using the restore function
3. If restore fails:
   - **Clear advantage**: Admin may award the game to the leading player
   - **No clear advantage**: Replay the full game with same civs and map
4. Do not watch the replay before rehosting

### Player Cannot Continue

If a player has technical issues preventing them from continuing:

- Contact admin immediately
- Admin will determine if rescheduling is possible
- Repeated technical issues may result in forfeit

## 14. Team Game Specific Rules

### Team Composition

- Team rosters should be declared before tournament starts
- Roster changes after signup require admin approval
- Replacement players must have equal or lower Elo than the player they replace

### Player Requirements

- All roster players should participate in at least 1 game during group stage
- Teams are encouraged to have 5+ players for scheduling flexibility

### Team Colors

Use standard team color assignments:

- **Team 1**: Blue (1), Green (3), Teal (5), Gray (7) — flanks are 1 and 7
- **Team 2**: Red (2), Yellow (4), Purple (6), Orange (8) — flanks are 2 and 8

Enable "Team Positions" in lobby to control spawn positions.

### Slinging Rules

**Default rule**: Slinging (sending resources to teammates) is allowed only when:

- The **sending player** is in a higher Age than the receiving player, OR
- Both sender and receiver are in **Imperial Age**

Tournament organizers may modify this rule, but must state it clearly.

### Team Communication

- Teams may use voice chat (Discord, etc.) during games
- No communication with anyone outside the team during games

## 15. Fair Play & Conduct

### Expected Behavior

All participants must:

- Show good sportsmanship at all times
- Treat opponents, admins, and casters with respect
- Cooperate with admins to resolve disputes
- Always play to win (no throwing games)

### Prohibited Behavior

- **Insults, harassment, or toxic behavior** — in-game, on Discord, or elsewhere
- **Racism, sexism, or discrimination** of any kind
- **Match fixing or betting** on games you're involved in
- **Seeding manipulation** — intentionally losing to get easier bracket
- **Impersonation** — playing under someone else's account

### Communication During Games

- Communication with anyone other than teammates and admins is **forbidden** during games
- This includes stream chat, spectators, and voice chat with non-teammates
- Violation is considered cheating

### Disputes

- If you believe an opponent violated rules, **pause and contact admin**
- Do not argue in-game; let admins handle disputes
- Admin decisions are final

## 16. Streaming & Broadcasting

### Player Streaming (POV)

- Players may stream their own POV **at their own risk**
- No stream delay is required, but players accept the risk of opponents potentially watching
- Stream chat and donations should not be visible/audible to the player during games

### Casting

- All tournament games may be cast by anyone
- Casters should credit the tournament and organizers
- Live casts must respect spectator delay
- For recorded game casts, results must not be spoiled before broadcast
- **If casters want to stream a game live**, players must cooperate and provide the game/lobby ID if casters have trouble spectating

### Stream Sniping

- Watching opponent's stream during a game is **strictly forbidden**
- This is considered cheating and results in immediate disqualification

### Match Visibility

- Matches are played **publicly** by default — anyone can spectate
- Private matches for later rec casting are rare and only used on special occasions with admin approval

## 17. Reporting Results

### How to Report

1. Complete all games in the set
2. **Rename your recorded game files** to format: `PlayerA vs PlayerB g1.aoe2record`, `PlayerA vs PlayerB g2.aoe2record`, etc.
3. **Pack all recorded games into a .zip file** (e.g., `PlayerA_vs_PlayerB.zip`)
4. Post in the **#recorded-games** channel on AoEBalkan Discord
5. Include the following information:
   - Player names
   - Map draft link (from aoe2cm.net)
   - Civ draft link (from aoe2cm.net)
   - **Result in spoiler tags** (use `||2-1||` format on Discord)
6. Attach the .zip file

### Example Format

```
PlayerA vs PlayerB
Maps: https://aoe2cm.net/draft/yMFrx
Civs: https://aoe2cm.net/draft/LWfKR
Result: ||2-1||
[PlayerA_vs_PlayerB.zip attached]
```

### How to Add Spoiler Tags on Discord

**Method 1 (typing)**: Wrap text in double pipes: `||2-1||` → displays as ||2-1||

**Method 2 (selection)**: 
1. Type your message with the result
2. Select/highlight the result text
3. Click the **eye icon** (👁) in the formatting toolbar that appears
4. The selected text becomes spoilered

### Recorded Games

- All recorded games must be kept and submitted
- **Anti-spoiler rule**: If the set ended before the maximum games (e.g., 2-0 in a BO3), include a dummy file for the unplayed game(s) — just copy and rename another replay. This prevents casters from knowing the result based on file count.
- Recordings may be requested by admins at any time
- Suspicious games will be reviewed

## 18. Prize Pool (If Applicable)

### Transparency Requirement

If a tournament has a prize pool, the organizer **must announce the following before signups close**:

1. **Starting prize pool amount** (even if it's expected to grow)
2. **Distribution percentages** for each placing (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.) — these stay fixed regardless of final amount
3. **Payment method** (PayPal, bank transfer, etc.)
4. **Expected payout timeline** (e.g., "within 2 weeks of tournament end")

If donations are accepted:
- Share a donation link (e.g., PayPal pool)
- Keep the community updated on the current total as it grows
- The final prize pool is whatever the total is when the tournament ends

### Recommended Distribution Examples

#### 1v1 Tournament (Top 4 paid)
| Place | Percentage |
|-------|------------|
| 1st | 50% |
| 2nd | 25% |
| 3rd | 15% |
| 4th | 10% |

#### 1v1 Tournament (Top 8 paid)
| Place | Percentage |
|-------|------------|
| 1st | 40% |
| 2nd | 20% |
| 3rd-4th | 12% each |
| 5th-8th | 4% each |

#### Team Tournament (Top 4 paid)
| Place | Percentage |
|-------|------------|
| 1st | 50% |
| 2nd | 30% |
| 3rd | 20% |

### Fair Distribution Principles

- Distribution should be announced **before** tournament starts, not after
- Don't change distribution mid-tournament unless all affected players agree
- Prize pool from entry fees should go entirely to players (minus any clearly stated costs)
- If using donations, be transparent about the current total

### Payout

- Organizers should pay out prizes **within 30 days** of tournament completion
- Communicate with winners about payment method preferences
- Keep records of payments made

## 19. Penalties & Enforcement

### Common Violations and Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Late to match (per 10 min) | Lose 1 game per 10 minutes late |
| Late to match (20+ min) | Opponent wins the set (unless reschedule agreed) |
| No-show without notice | Opponent wins the set + potential disqualification |
| Stream sniping | Disqualification |
| Wrong civ picked | Restart, opponent picks your civ |
| Wrong lobby settings | Restart (no penalty if caught early) |
| Laming violation (if rules apply) | Game loss |
| Nomad rule violation | Game loss |
| Using macros/cheats | Disqualification + potential ban |
| Toxic behavior | Warning → temporary ban → permanent ban |
| Match fixing | Disqualification + permanent ban |

### Escalation

1. **First offense (minor)**: Warning
2. **Second offense or major violation**: Game/set loss
3. **Repeated violations or severe misconduct**: Disqualification
4. **Cheating or match fixing**: Permanent ban from community tournaments

### Admin Discretion

Admins may apply any penalty they deem appropriate. This includes situations not explicitly covered by rules.

## 20. Admin Rights & Responsibilities

### Admin Rights

- Amend, remove, or change rules as needed
- Make judgment calls on situations not covered by rules
- Apply penalties as appropriate
- Restart or award games based on evidence

### Admin Responsibilities

- Be available during scheduled match times
- Respond to player inquiries promptly
- Apply rules consistently and fairly
- Document all rulings and penalties
- Remain impartial (admins should not participate as players in tournaments they're running)

### Conflicts of Interest

- If an organizer or admin is also playing, they must **recuse themselves** from any decisions involving their own matches
- Another admin must handle disputes involving admin-players

### Appeals

- Players may appeal admin decisions to the head organizer
- Keep appeals reasonable — we're a community, not a court
- Final decisions rest with the tournament organizer

## Appendix A: Quick Reference Card

### Draft Host Determination
- Higher seed = Host for both drafts
- In playoffs, better placement = Host

### Punctuality
- Every 10 min late = lose 1 game
- 20 min late = opponent wins the set (unless they agree to reschedule)

### Restarts
- 1 free restart per set (first 5 minutes)
- Admin restarts for bugged maps (don't count against you)

### Nomad Rules (Custom maps without built-in treaty, first 3 min)
- No attacking
- No walling enemy resources
- Stealing herdables OK
- TC in range of enemy TC = immediate restart

### Slinging (Team Games)
- OK if sender is higher Age, OR both in Imperial
